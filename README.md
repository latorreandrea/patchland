# Patchland

> Res Publica Mundi — A distributed, imaginary nation built one Patch at a time.[cite: 2]

Patchland is a collaborative world-building project where people voluntarily dedicate a small portion of a place they legitimately use (garden, yard, balcony, etc.) as a **Patch**.[cite: 2]

The project is purely symbolic and recreational, with no legal, political, or territorial claims.[cite: 2]

## 📐 Requirements for a Patch

1. **Availability of the Space:** The Custodian must legitimately have access to the space (ownership, rental, permission, etc.).[cite: 2]
2. **Minimum Size:** Minimum area of **1 square meter (1 m²)**.[cite: 2]
3. **Defined Boundaries:** The Patch must have clearly identifiable limits.[cite: 2]
4. **Official Name:** Every Patch has a unique name (e.g., *Raven Grove*, *Aurora Garden*, *Patch 51*).[cite: 2]
5. **Registration:** Each Patch records its Name, Custodian, Coordinates, Area, Foundation Date, Province, optional photos, and a short history.[cite: 2]
6. **Official Seal (Mandatory):** Each Patch owns a unique, numbered, and personalized seal registered in the National Archive to stamp visitors' passports.[cite: 2]
7. **Story:** Every Patch tells a story, even if only a single sentence.[cite: 2]

## 🏛️ The Custodian, Citizenship & Tourism

* **The Custodian:** Responsible for maintaining information, preserving the seal, and welcoming visitors when possible.[cite: 2]
* **Citizenship:** Anyone who registers at least one Patch becomes a citizen.[cite: 2] Symbolic titles may depend on maintained area, contribution, seniority, or community participation.[cite: 2]
* **Passport:** Citizens can obtain a Patchland Passport to collect Patch stamps, commemorative event marks, and visited provinces.[cite: 2]
* **Tourism & Access:** Each Patch can set its visibility and access level:
  * **Public**[cite: 2]
  * **Visit on request**[cite: 2]
  * **Private**[cite: 2]
