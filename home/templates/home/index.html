Vision

What is Patchland?

Patchland is a collaborative world-building project in which people voluntarily dedicate a small portion of a place they legitimately use — a garden, a yard, a balcony — as a Patch.

The project is purely symbolic and recreational: it has no legal, political or territorial claims. What remains is a shared, imaginary nation — Res Publica Mundi — held together by stories.

Requirements

Founding a Patch

Seven simple rules turn a square meter of the world into a piece of the nation.

1 {% include 'partials/icon.html' with name='home' class='size-6 text-brass-300' %}

Availability of the space

The Custodian must legitimately have availability — ownership, rental or permission.

2 {% include 'partials/icon.html' with name='arrows-pointing-out' class='size-6 text-brass-300' %}

Minimum size

At least 1 square meter (1 m²).

3 {% include 'partials/icon.html' with name='map-pin' class='size-6 text-brass-300' %}

Defined boundaries

The Patch must have clearly identifiable limits.

4 {% include 'partials/icon.html' with name='pencil' class='size-6 text-brass-300' %}

Official name

Every Patch has a unique name, such as Raven Grove or Aurora Garden.

5 {% include 'partials/icon.html' with name='clipboard-document-list' class='size-6 text-brass-300' %}

Registration

Name, Custodian, coordinates, area, foundation date, province, photos and a short history.

6 {% include 'partials/icon.html' with name='check-badge' class='size-6 text-brass-300' %}

Official Seal (mandatory)

A unique seal, registered in the National Archive, used to stamp visitors' passports.

7 {% include 'partials/icon.html' with name='book-open' class='size-6 text-brass-300' %}

Story

Every Patch should tell a story, even if only a single sentence.

{% include 'partials/icon.html' with name='user-circle' class='size-6' %}

World Map & Provinces

One territory, scattered everywhere

Patches are grouped into Provinces. The interactive map displays every registered Patch together with the total area, the citizens, the provinces and the countries represented.

There is no capital and no centre — the territory simply lives wherever a Patch has been founded.
