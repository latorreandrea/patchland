The Manifesto

A distributed, imaginary nation built one Patch at a time — purely symbolic, purely recreational, with no legal, political or territorial claims.

“One Patch. One Citizen. One World.”

Vision

Why Patchland exists

Patchland is a collaborative world-building project in which people voluntarily dedicate a small portion of a place they legitimately use — a garden, a yard, a balcony — as a Patch.

Every Patch is a tiny, real piece of the world that chooses to belong to a shared imaginary nation. The whole country is the sum of its squares: Ex multis, una terra — out of many, one land.

A nation that grows one square meter at a time, wherever its citizens live — no borders, no claims, just stories.
