# PATCHLAND

## *Res Publica Mundi*

> **Concept:** A distributed, imaginary nation built one Patch at a
> time.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Vision

Patchland is a collaborative world-building project in which people
voluntarily dedicate a small portion of a place they legitimately use
(garden, yard, balcony, etc.) as a **Patch**.

The project is purely symbolic and recreational. It has no legal,
political or territorial claims.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Principles

-   One Patch at a time.
-   Every Patch has a story.
-   Every citizen contributes equally.
-   The nation is distributed across the world.
-   Community comes before territory.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Objectives

-   Build the world's largest distributed symbolic nation.
-   Connect people through meaningful places.
-   Encourage exploration and tourism between Patchland citizens.
-   Preserve the history of every Patch.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Requirements for a Patch

## 1. Availability of the Space

The Custodian must legitimately have the availability of the space
(ownership, rental, permission, etc.).

## 2. Minimum Size

Minimum area: **1 square meter (1 m²)**.

## 3. Defined Boundaries

The Patch must have clearly identifiable limits.

## 4. Official Name

Every Patch has a unique name.

Examples: - Raven Grove - Aurora Garden - Patch 51

## 5. Registration

Each Patch records: - Name - Custodian - Coordinates - Area - Foundation
date - Province - Optional photos - Short history

## 6. Official Seal (Mandatory)

Each Patch owns a unique seal.

The seal: - identifies the Patch; - is registered in the National
Archive; - is used to stamp visitors' passports.

## 7. Story

Every Patch should tell a story, even if only a single sentence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# The Custodian

Every Patch has a Custodian responsible for: - maintaining
information; - preserving the seal; - welcoming visitors when possible.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Passport

Every citizen may obtain a Patchland Passport.

The passport collects: - Patch stamps; - commemorative events; -
provinces visited.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Tourism

Each Patch can be:

-   Public
-   Visit on request
-   Private

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Official Seal

Every seal is: - unique; - numbered; - personalized; - permanently
linked to a single Patch.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# World Map

The map displays: - all registered Patches; - total area; - citizens; -
provinces; - countries represented.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Provinces

Patches are grouped into Provinces.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Citizenship

Anyone who registers at least one Patch becomes a citizen.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Titles

seniority; - community participation.
